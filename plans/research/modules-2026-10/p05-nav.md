## Contradicts or bugs

- `WalkFacts.StepUp = 0.45` (ours) comes from his `Facts.WalkStepUp`, which his own attribute marks "Unmeasured: no live step course has measured Character's real limit". We copied an unmeasured number as fact. Sure (95%) that it is unmeasured; unsure what the real value is. Idea 21 would measure it.
- `JumpClear = 1.2` (our `HopMax`) is the measured jump peak 2.21 m minus a 1 m margin for the forward motion and capsule; it is a planning figure, not a game limit. A hop onto a 1.3 m top from a run-up may work. Sure (90%) it is a fudge by his own comment.
- His claim that the game's Humanoid navmesh agent climbs 85 deg and walls shut doors is from his own live runs and a decompile; I cannot confirm it for vanilla. If true, our `nav_path` inherits both (idea 7). Confidence 55%.
- `Shelter.Roofed(Fireplace)` tests the roof 1.5 m above the fire pivot with a fixed offset. The game's Fireplace wet check uses its own per-prefab `m_coverCheckOffset`, so a fixed 1.5 m can disagree for low fires. Confidence 60%, not verified.
- "Creatures cannot open doors" (basis of his enclosure test) is my belief too, but it is not proven here; some mobs may path through a door another way. Confidence 75%. Our `enclosure` output should say "with doors shut" and not claim safety.
- Piece usage tags (`Piece.UsageTagFlags` "Floor" / "Stairs") come from his game build 1.0.17 decompile. I could not tell whether they exist in other builds. Check with `inspect_type Piece` before relying on them (idea 10). Confidence that the fields exist in his build: high; in ours: unknown.
- `Fireplace.CheckUnderTerrain` as "a solid 0.5..1.0 m straight above the pivot" is his reading of a terrain-check function; the name suggests terrain, not any collider. Confidence 55%. Check before copying (idea 13).
- His design-rule numbers (lip 0.15, station corridor 2.0 / 0.4, storey 4 m, eave 2.5 m, walkway 1.5 m) are the user's preferences ("user design rule"), not Valheim limits. Mark them as warnings, never errors.
- `Climb = 1.2` as the "ground" band of a grid is a modelling choice (the real controller has no such band); the layered grids hide the seams, but a floor 1.2-1.9 m above the reference reads as "chest height" and blocks.
