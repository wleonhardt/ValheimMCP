## Contradicts or bugs
Ours (found while comparing; code facts are certain, game facts as marked)
- `bed_check` `canSleep` = cover >= 0.8 && roof. It ignores the Heat-area rule (confidence 85% that sleeping needs a nearby fire in vanilla) and uses origin + 0.9 for the spawn point (60% that `Bed.GetSpawnPoint` is the better point).
- Walker arrival and aim are x,z only (Control.cs `Tick`): certain; leads to arriving under floors and last-metre spin.
- `RiseAhead` rays use mask ~0 and no slope correction: certain; effect on Hop/RunUp order is probable (rise at 1 m on a 45 deg bank is ~1.0, inside Hop's window).
- SideStep and `HazardAhead` rays/overlaps ignore triggers for the strafe path: certain; a portal trigger is a trigger collider.
- `Reroute` uses the "Humanoid" agent for the full remaining route: certain; the game-side facts (slope 85, swims, tiles poked only at the ends) are his decompile, ~70% sure.
- `plan_check` has dead `NotOnWood/NotOnFloor/GroundOnly` fields and, on the server, only generator ground (documented in `groundNote`).
- `portals` text "Every portal in the world" is wrong on a client (65% sure, his decompile of ZDOMan.CreateSyncList).
- Journal has no world key (certain).
His docs and code
- His own docs disagree: run drain 8/s vs 10/s, swim 6->3/s vs 5->2/s, base stamina 75 vs 50, day length 1200 vs 1800, BlockedbyPlayer players vs any Character, wisp torch 24 vs 12 m (he marks U), core wood reach 24 m vs 20-22 m (section 10 uses a 0.8 factor; the table gives 12 pieces = 24 m, so 24 m is right).
- ReplySanitizer cheat regex read "the spawn - does" as a console command and refused a plain question (odev tour 13): false positive in his code, certain from the text.
- `World.Doors.IsDeck` tested a flat AABB and called a gate sill a drawbridge (regression, fixed): lesson for any "deck" test: use the panel's thin axis after rotation.
- Presence-as-state (a file's existence turned the routine ON), `Ctx.Stop` that does not stop, and escape-hatch bool flags (`Admit`, `Basin`, `force`, `only`) are the root causes of his regressions; our code has none of these in the same shape, but `dry` skipping the write gate once did (fixed in 0.5.0).
- Sources are wiki and Steam threads for many numbers marked [W]; treat every combat and 1.0 value as unverified.
