## Contradicts or bugs

- His crafting maths take the FIRST enabled recipe only and read ingredients with quality 1. Recipes with alternatives (Bronze x1 vs x5) are picked by list order, `m_requireOnlyOneIngredient` ("any one of" recipes) would be costed as "all of", and upgrade costs and station levels are not modelled. I am fairly sure the `m_requireOnlyOneIngredient` field exists in Valheim's Recipe (about 75 percent); the first-recipe and quality-1 limits are certain from his code.
- He says the `NoCraftCost` key also removes the station-LEVEL check, and that the no-cost cheat still consumes the inventory when placing. Both come from his 1.0.16 decompile notes. I could not check either: our `ext/lib` Assembly-CSharp.dll is a 23 KB stub. The second one contradicts the older game behaviour I know (cheat skipped consumption), so verify against our own decompile before copying (confidence he is right: about 60 percent).
- Expected yields assume resource rate 1 (his own doc says so); rows from `item_sources` are not scaled by `Game.m_resourceRate`. Low impact, but a copy of the idea must scale or print the rate.
- `docs/resources.md` has a spliced duplicate fragment in the `gather` table row (text repeated mid-sentence); documentation only.
- Tool tiers (flint axe 1, birch/oak min tier 2, "Fir gives no core wood") are read live in his notes; do not hard-code them in our tools, read them from the prefabs (sure).
- His smelter maths looked correct against the game rules I know (first matching conversion, OnAddFuel refusing above max-1, fuel per product). No defect found there. Windmill power is divided into the time exactly as the bake timer is multiplied by it (consistent).
