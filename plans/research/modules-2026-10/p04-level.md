## Ours vs his on terrain (what is missing or wrong)

- Missing: any terrain WRITE tool; any pure terrain model (hoe/pickaxe/cultivator/path ops, ZoneBase, collider-triangle height); pad/levelability feasibility; original-ground and delta readout; slide/approach checks; cultivated/cleared verdicts; site ranking.

## Contradicts or bugs

- His docs say `Heightmap.GetHeight(Vector3)` returns the NEAREST vertex (a "banned call"). I remember it as bilinear in vanilla. Confidence 55 % that he is wrong; irrelevant to us as long as we use `ZoneSystem.GetGroundHeight` (ray).
- Dry thresholds are inconsistent in his own code: stands 30.4 (DryGroundY), pad minimum sea+1 = 31, site hard minimum 31.5, research doc says `h < 28.5` for swim. All soft; treat 30.4 as the shore wash and do not copy 31.5 as a game rule. Sure (high) it is his heuristic, not a game fact.
- `Water.Floor` (wade floor 28.9 = 30 - 1.1) vs `Water.Feet` (float height 28.5 = 30 - 1.5): both come from one decompiled rule (depth over feet > max(0, swimDepth - 0.4)); consistent, but easy to misuse as one number.
- ScaffoldPlanner uses MinSupport 20 and derived multipliers (0.638 stair, 0.564 landing, 0.7375 pole, 0.75 post side) while the game breaks at 10; intentional margin, but the multipliers are unverified hand derivations (I could not reproduce 0.638 and 0.564 from loss 0.2/0.125 and COM 2.24 m). Medium-low confidence they are exact.
- Enclosure doc says creatures "cannot open doors": true for most monsters as far as I know, but not verified for every AI (Dvergr, goblins); treat as unverified (50 %).
- `Terraces.DigWeight = 3` and the site weights are invented tuning, not game facts. Do not present them as rules.
- In `Sweep.Outcome` a terrain hit that is skipped (`!r.Digs` false or flag4 set) falls into the `else flag4 = true` branch: harmless today, but easy to misread; the pickaxe rule it encodes (terrain digs only when no object came first) is the interesting part and rests on one live observation of "no damage" swings.
- His `TerrainComp` constants (levelDelta clamp +-8 AND final clamp base +-8, smoothDelta +-1) come from decompile line refs in `Facts.cs`; I could not check them. Marked "Decomp" there, so treat as likely, not proven.
