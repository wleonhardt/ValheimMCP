## Contradicts or bugs

- Food.WantsBite comment says the food bonus "decays as it burns". I believe the bonus stays at full value until the last seconds, but I am not sure (about 55 %). His own doc says "value still 0.81 x" at half burn, which I cannot explain. A cheap live check: read player_status foods and max health over one food's life.
- World/Farm.cs PlotSpot comment says ReadyIn is m_growTimeMax less the time since planting; FarmReader uses Plant.GetGrowTime() (per-plant, seeded by position, between m_growTime and m_growTimeMax). The code is right, the comment is stale (certain, internal).
- docs/survival.md says the reference numbers are decompiled 1.0.16 while other lines say 1.0.17; several species numbers (pen caps, love points, Hare in Mistlands) are marked "live 2026-10-06" and were not re-checked here. Treat them as claims, read them from prefabs.
- Comfort replay compares each piece with the previous piece in the sorted list, counted or not. I recall the game keeps the last counted piece. I could not find a case where the two differ for real comfort pieces (different groups sort apart), so low risk (about 70 % sure of my recollection).
- Resting rule "not Cold/Freezing" (docs/survival.md comfort_state note): plausible but I cannot confirm it from memory (about 60 %). Verify with player_status before relying on it.
- CharacterDrop expectation uses 2^(level-1) for both amount and chance (cited CharacterDrop.cs:73-131). I remember a linear level multiplier but am unsure (about 50 %). Check against a live kill count before using it.
- No error found in the rest/shelter/bed thresholds (0.8 bed and shelter, 0.7 station and egg, 30 s sleep cooldown, 300 s + 60 s per level Rested, 3 food slots, re-eat at half burn): they agree with what I know of the game.
