# Package 2 (fight, threat, attacks, creatures): ideas for ValheimMCP

Read: all of the pack except the bot-loop bodies (see "Not read"). Extra files read from the clone because the special focus needs them: VhExt/World/BiomeRisk.cs, VhExt/World/Facts.cs (threat/windup/sense sections), docs/reference/valheim-knowledge.md sections 5 and 11. ## Contradicts or bugs

- Night range disagrees inside his own repo. `BiomeRisk.Window` uses day 0.25..0.75 of the day fraction (night = up to 0.25 or from 0.75). His knowledge doc, Mistlands section, says mechanical night is 20:24-03:36 (day fraction 0.85 to 0.15). One of the two is wrong for `EnvMan` day/night; I am not sure which (50/50 from memory). Check `EnvMan.IsDay/IsNight` and what `SpawnSystem` uses for `m_spawnAtDay/Night` before coding idea 9.
- `Creatures.Facts` computes a creature's hit with armour only (`Combat().OnUs(raw x level factor, GetBodyArmor())`) and ignores the player's own per-type resistances (frost or fire mead, gear). `BiomeRisk.HitOn` does apply them. So the danger tiers can overstate a hit that a resistance mead cuts (confident, about 85 %). Matters if we copy the tier rule: take the resistances as an input.
- `RemoteTargetsUs`: for a creature another client owns, "targets us" is `haveTarget` and closing at 0.8 m/s or more. `haveTarget` means any target, so with two players a creature walking toward the other player counts as targeting you. It is a heuristic and he says so; do not present it as a fact in a tool (confident, about 90 %).
- `Live.Creatures.AttackOf` caches the attack read per creature in a static dictionary on first sight. If the creature was scanned in the frame it loaded, before the Humanoid has given itself its default items, the cache holds an empty read (no hit known, reach falls back to 2.5 m, even for an archer) for the rest of its life. Low-medium confidence (about 45 %): I did not see where `GiveDefaultItems` runs. Do not cache an empty result in our reader.
- `Facts.AttackWindups` is a table of seeded guesses (his own tag says "Unmeasured": greydwarf 0.6 s, troll club 1.2 s and so on). Not a game fact; the real hit moment is an animation event. Do not adopt the table; at most record windups measured by `Humanoid.OnAttackTrigger` after the InAttack edge.
- Reach: `MeleeRange + c.GetRadius()` uses the attacker's radius. I recall `MonsterAI` subtracting the target's radius from the distance before comparing with `m_aiAttackRange`; not sure (about 35 % that his form is wrong). For a toolbox the error is under a metre.
- `RawHit` sums all damage types except chop and pickaxe and applies armour to the total. That matches "armour on the total after per-type resistance" in his BiomeRisk comment (the same function is cited under two names, `HitData.DamageTypes.ApplyArmor` and `HitData.ApplyArmor`). I think it is right (about 70 %); confirm in our decompile once, since idea 3 depends on it.
- `ReadFoes` drops creatures that are swimming or deeper than a wade (`Hunt.OnFoot`) from the fight list, but the danger scan still counts them. Intentional, but a "who can reach me" tool must pick one rule on purpose.
- Wiki-sourced numbers in his doc that conflict with his own live data are not marked: PlayerBase radii "workbench 20 m + 4 m per upgrade" looks like a wiki guess; he lists it as an open question in the same section ("does the spawn-block sphere grow with workbench level"). Treat every number tagged [W] or [U] as unverified.
